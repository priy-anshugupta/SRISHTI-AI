SECTION 8: OPERATIONAL INCIDENTS & NPT RECORD (PAGE 82)
DATE: 05-AUG-2019 | DEPTH: 2,540m MD | FORMATION: Tipam Sandstone (TS-3)
INCIDENT: Severe Lost Circulation

DESCRIPTION:
While drilling 8-1/2" hole at 2,540m MD with 11.2 ppg WBM at 620 GPM, complete loss of returns occurred upon penetrating micro-fractured porous Tipam TS-3 interval. Flow rate dropped from 100% to 35% returns within 15 minutes. Active pit volume dropped by 38 bbl. Loss rate estimated at 60 bbl/hr.

MITIGATION ACTION & RESOLUTION:
1. Immediately pulled off bottom to 2,500m MD to avoid packing off BHA.
2. Reduced pump rate to minimum circulating speed (30 SPM / 250 GPM).
3. Mixed 25 bbl LCM pill: Coarse Calcium Carbonate (CaCO3) 30 ppb + Medium Mica 15 ppb in slug tank.
4. Spotted pill across loss zone (2,520m–2,560m) and squeezed with 250 psi annular pressure.
5. Monitored well for 2 hours — static fluid level stable.
6. Reduced mud weight from 11.2 ppg to 10.4 ppg to stay within 10.2–10.6 ppg safe window.
7. Resumed drilling with full circulation restored. Zero seepage losses.
8. Total NPT: 48 hours (2 days). Cost impact: INR 1.5 Crore.

OPERATIONAL LESSON:
In Tipam Sandstone TS-3 to TS-5 (2,400m–2,700m typical), pore pressure gradient is normal hydrostatic (0.44 psi/ft). Equivalent MW exceeding 10.8 ppg induces micro-fracture propagation in permeable braided sands. Maintain MW within 10.2–10.6 ppg window. Keep 30 bbl coarse CaCO3 pill pre-mixed in standby slug tank before entering Tipam TS-3.
