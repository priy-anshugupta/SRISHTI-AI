SECTION 1: GEOLOGICAL CONTEXT
The Upper Assam Basin is a compressional foreland basin bounded by:
- Naga Thrust Belt (NE) — active collision zone
- Mikir Hills (S) — Precambrian cratonic basement high
- Brahmaputra River alluvial plain (W) — subsidence zone
The Baghjan field lies on a NW-SE trending anticlinal structure within the Central Shelf trend.

SECTION 3: KEY FINDINGS
1. KOPILI FORMATION is the critical drilling hazard: narrow MW window of only 0.9 ppg (10.15-10.8 ppg equivalent). At well deviations >25°, the collapse MW rises sharply to 81 pcf.
2. PRESSURE REVERSAL observed below Kopili: pore pressure drops from 74 pcf to 68 pcf entering Sylhet Limestone, then rises sharply again to 82 pcf in Lakadong/Therria.
3. Recommended CASING STRATEGY: Set 9-5/8" intermediate casing shoe deep into Kopili Formation to achieve high LOT before drilling narrow-window lower section.

SECTION 4: IN-SITU STRESS REGIME (from Alam et al. 2019, GJI)
Wells M1, M2, M3 (Moran) and N1 (Nahorkatiya):
- Vertical Stress (Sv): 21.7 - 23.8 MPa/km
- Max Horizontal Stress (SH): 14.9 - 23.1 MPa/km
- Min Horizontal Stress (Sh): 12.0 - 21.4 MPa/km
- Stress Regime: Normal to Strike-Slip transitional (Sh < Sv < SH in some intervals)
- Tectonic strain causes horizontal stress rotation up to 20° near major fault blocks.
